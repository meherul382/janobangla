CAMPAIGN NOTES
- Start with exact and phrase match for tighter control.
- Use the most relevant article/category as each ad group's landing page.
- Do not send every ad to the homepage if a specific article is more relevant.
- Check the Search Terms report regularly and add genuinely irrelevant terms as negatives.
- Do not use bots, click farms, incentivized clicks, or purchased fake traffic.
